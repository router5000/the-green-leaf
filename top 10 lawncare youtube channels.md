# top 10 lawncare youtube channels

Here are the top 10 lawncare YouTube channels, widely recommended for a mix of expert advice, equipment reviews, DIY tutorials, and business insights in lawn maintenance:[^1_1][^1_2][^1_5][^1_7]

### Top 10 Lawncare YouTube Channels

1. **Lawn Care Juggernaut**
Known for dramatic overgrown property transformations and practical advice. Over 760K subscribers.[^1_5][^1_9]
2. **Top Notch Lawn Care**
Real-world landscaping projects and clean-ups, operated by Brian. Over 340K subscribers.[^1_6][^1_5]
3. **Ryan Knorr Lawn Care**
Popular for clear, high-quality technique tutorials and homeowner-focused guides. Over 170K subscribers.[^1_2][^1_3]
4. **Brian’s Lawn Maintenance**
Mixes equipment reviews, business advice, and mowing technique tips. Over 245K subscribers.[^1_1][^1_2]
5. **Lawn Care Life**
Focus on weed control/fertilization and helping people start their business. Over 76K subscribers.[^1_8][^1_2]
6. **Blades of Grass Lawn Care**
Savannah, Georgia-based channel with tips, product reviews, and service demos. Over 70K subscribers.[^1_2][^1_6]
7. **Grass Daddy**
Hundreds of videos dedicated to homeowner-centric lawn care and product reviews. Over 56K subscribers.[^1_2]
8. **Main Street Mower**
Family business offering power equipment repairs and reviews. Over 48K subscribers.[^1_1]
9. **Weed Whacker Lawn Care**
Residential lawncare with 26 years of experience–practical, straightforward advice. Over 29K subscribers.[^1_1]
10. **Lawn Care Millionaire**
Business-building strategies and tutorials for growing a successful lawn care operation.[^1_7]

### Notable Mentions

- **Elite Lawn Care**: Entertaining, general advice-focused channel, especially for Oklahoma City region.[^1_7]
- **Al Badez**: Quickly gaining popularity for innovative content in the new wave of lawncare creators.[^1_7]
- **Turf Mechanic**: Specialized advice focusing on eco-friendly and technical aspects.[^1_3]
- **The Grass Factor**: More advanced, chemistry-driven explanations for seasoned professionals.[^1_3]

These channels offer broad, well-produced content catering to beginners, homeowners, and lawncare professionals, making them ideal choices for anyone looking to improve their lawn care skills or business.[^1_5][^1_3][^1_2][^1_7]
