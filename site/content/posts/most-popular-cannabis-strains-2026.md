### Blue Dream — The Enduring All-Rounder

A cross of Blueberry and Haze, Blue Dream (17–24% THC) has remained one of the top-searched and top-selling strains in the U.S. for nearly a decade[[1]](#user-content-fn-1). Its dominant terpene, myrcene, is associated with an earthy, herbal aroma and contributes to a smooth, full-body sensation that stops short of heavy sedation[[3]](#user-content-fn-3).

Blue Dream is an ideal starting point for newer consumers and a reliable daily driver for experienced users who want balance.

### OG Kush — The Foundation of Modern Cannabis

OG Kush (20–26% THC) is arguably the most influential strain in modern cannabis genetics — Runtz, Gelato, and Wedding Cake all trace lineage back to it[[4]](#user-content-fn-4). Its pungent fuel-and-pine aroma comes largely from beta-caryophyllene and limonene.

Caryophyllene is notable among terpenes because it also interacts with CB2 receptors in the endocannabinoid system[[5]](#user-content-fn-5), which researchers believe may contribute to its distinctive physical effect profile.

### Runtz — The Candy-Coated Cultural Phenomenon

A cross of Zkittlez and Gelato, Runtz (22–29% THC) exploded in popularity around 2020 and shows no sign of slowing down[[1]](#user-content-fn-1). Its candy-sweet flavor profile, driven by limonene and caryophyllene, pairs with a balanced euphoric high that appeals to both recreational and wellness-minded consumers.

The strain is available in dozens of phenotype expressions — White Runtz, Pink Runtz, and Runtz OG among them — giving dispensary shoppers plenty of variety within the family.

### Jealousy — The New Elite Strain to Know

Jealousy (Sherbert Bx1 × Gelato 41) has rapidly risen from boutique genetics to mainstream bestseller[[2]](#user-content-fn-2). Testing consistently at 24–30% THC with a terpene profile heavy in caryophyllene and limonene, it delivers a creamy, dessert-forward flavor followed by a heavy, relaxing effect onset.

Jealousy represents the 2026 trend toward "connoisseur genetics" — strains bred as much for flavor complexity as for potency[[4]](#user-content-fn-4).

### Gelato #33, Gary Payton, and Wedding Cake

These three strains round out the top seven and each fills a distinct consumer niche. Gelato #33 (20–25% THC) offers a balanced mood lift suited to creative activities[[2]](#user-content-fn-2). Gary Payton (20–28% THC) skews energetic and mentally stimulating, making it a popular daytime choice. Wedding Cake (22–27% THC) leans toward evening relaxation with a sweet, vanilla-dough aroma driven by myrcene.

## What Terpenes Are Driving Cannabis Strain Popularity in 2026?

Consumers are increasingly shopping by terpene profile, not just by THC percentage[[2]](#user-content-fn-2). Here's a quick breakdown of the three terpenes most commonly found in 2026's top strains:

- **Myrcene** — The most abundant terpene in cannabis overall[[3]](#user-content-fn-3). Earthy and herbal in aroma, myrcene is associated with relaxing, sedating qualities and appears prominently in Blue Dream and Wedding Cake.
- **Beta-Caryophyllene** — A spicy, peppery terpene unique in that it binds directly to CB2 receptors in the endocannabinoid system[[5]](#user-content-fn-5). Found in OG Kush, Jealousy, and Gelato #33.
- **Limonene** — Bright and citrusy, limonene is present in Runtz, Gary Payton, and OG Kush. It is associated with uplifting, mood-brightening effects in consumer reports[[3]](#user-content-fn-3).

Research published in peer-reviewed cannabis science journals suggests that terpenes may work synergistically with cannabinoids — a concept often called the "entourage effect" — though scientists note that more human clinical research is needed to fully characterize these interactions[[5]](#user-content-fn-5)[[6]](#user-content-fn-6).

## How to Choose the Right Strain From This List

Choosing a strain is personal. THC percentage is a starting point, but it's only one variable. Here's a practical framework to narrow your choice[[6]](#user-content-fn-6):

- **If you're new to cannabis or have a lower tolerance**, start with strains in the 17–22% THC range, like Blue Dream or Gelato #33
- **If you prioritize flavor and aroma**, look for strains with diverse terpene certificates of analysis (COA) — Jealousy and Runtz consistently score well
- **If you want daytime energy and focus**, sativa-leaning hybrids like Gary Payton or Blue Dream are strong choices
- **If you prefer evening relaxation**, indica-leaning hybrids like Wedding Cake, OG Kush, or Jealousy tend to deliver heavier body effects
- **Always read the product's COA** from your dispensary — real terpene and cannabinoid data beats any general strain description
