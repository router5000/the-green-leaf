# Amnesia Haze Strain Profile: Effects, Terpenes & Lineage

## Quick Answer

[Amnesia Haze](/strains/amnesia-haze) is a legendary sativa-dominant hybrid known for its powerful cerebral euphoria, energizing effects, and complex citrus-forward aroma. Typically testing between 20–25% THC, it's a favorite among experienced consumers seeking creativity and uplift. Its multi-continent lineage and Cannabis Cup pedigree have made it one of the most recognized strains in the world.

## What Is the Origin and Genetics of Amnesia Haze?

Amnesia Haze has roots that span multiple continents, reflecting the globetrotting breeding history of the original Haze family. The strain is believed to descend from a cross of classic Haze genetics with landrace strains including Afghani, Hawaiian, and South Asian indica varieties[[1]](#user-content-fn-1). Some accounts also include Jamaican and Cambodian genetics in the mix, though lineage records from the Amsterdam coffee shop scene — where Amnesia Haze rose to prominence in the 1990s — are not always precise.

What is consistent is its legacy. Amnesia Haze took home first place at the High Times Cannabis Cup in 2004 and again in 2012, validating its place among elite sativa-dominant cultivars[[3]](#user-content-fn-3). Dutch breeders, particularly those operating in Amsterdam, are widely credited with refining the strain into the consistent, high-performing version consumers know today.

## What Does Amnesia Haze Look, Smell, and Taste Like?

Amnesia Haze buds are characteristically long and fluffy — a hallmark of sativa-dominant genetics. The flowers are a bright, lime green with amber-orange pistils winding throughout, and a generous coating of silvery-white trichomes that signal the strain's potency[[1]](#user-content-fn-1).

The aroma is one of Amnesia Haze's most celebrated traits. Terpinolene, its lead terpene, delivers a fresh, citrusy profile with floral and slightly herbal undertones[[2]](#user-content-fn-2). Myrcene adds a touch of earthiness beneath the brightness, while ocimene contributes a sweet, tropical lift. On the palate, expect lemon zest and earthy spice on the inhale, with a smooth, slightly sweet finish.

This terpene combination — terpinolene, myrcene, and ocimene — is relatively rare and helps distinguish Amnesia Haze from other citrus-forward strains like [Lemon Haze](/strains/lemon-haze) or [Super Lemon Haze](/strains/super-lemon-haze)[[4]](#user-content-fn-4).

## What Effects Does Amnesia Haze Produce?

Amnesia Haze earns its name from its head-heavy, sometimes disorienting euphoria — not from any literal memory loss. Onset is typically fast, especially when smoked or vaped, with a rush of uplifting energy and mental clarity arriving within minutes[[1]](#user-content-fn-1). Users commonly report:

- An immediate mood lift and sense of happiness
- Heightened creativity and free-associative thinking
- Increased sociability and talkativeness
- A light, buzzy body sensation without heavy sedation

Effects tend to last 2–3 hours, with the cerebral peak hitting hard and early. Because of its high THC ceiling — some cuts test at or above 25% — new consumers or those sensitive to THC may find the experience overwhelming[[5]](#user-content-fn-5). Reported side effects include anxiety, paranoia, dry mouth, and dry eyes, particularly at higher doses.

This is firmly a daytime strain. Its energizing profile makes it poorly suited for evening wind-down, but an excellent companion for creative projects, outdoor activities, or social settings.

## How Does Amnesia Haze Compare to Similar Strains?

Consumers who enjoy Amnesia Haze tend to also gravitate toward other Haze-family cultivars. Here's how it stacks up:

- **[Super Silver Haze](/strains/super-silver-haze)** – Similar sativa energy and Haze lineage, slightly more balanced, with a spicier aroma profile
- **Lemon Haze** – Shares the citrus-forward flavor but typically sits lower in THC (around 17–20%) and feels slightly less intense[[1]](#user-content-fn-1)
- **[Green Crack](/strains/green-crack)** – A comparable daytime energizer, but with a sharper, more aggressive mental stimulation and mango-forward [terpenes](/articles/understanding-cannabis-certificates-of-analysis)
- **Ghost Train Haze** – For consumers who want to push the THC ceiling even further, though with a more psychedelic edge

Amnesia Haze sits in a sweet spot for sativa lovers: powerful but not fringe, complex but approachable for experienced users[[6]](#user-content-fn-6).

## Conclusion

Amnesia Haze is a benchmark sativa-dominant hybrid — a strain that rewards experienced consumers with an invigorating, creativity-forward experience built on decades of refined genetics. Its terpinolene-rich aroma, high THC range, and consistent euphoric effects make it a go-to for daytime use when energy and mental engagement are the goal. If you're newer to cannabis or sensitive to THC, consider starting with a milder sativa before working your way up to this Cannabis Cup legend. For seasoned consumers ready for a full-throttle cerebral ride, Amnesia Haze consistently delivers.
