# focus on forums, not reddit... and youtube videos.... I'm looking for places to share the link to my lawncare blog

The best non‑Reddit places to share a lawn care blog link are classic lawn forums and pro‑oriented discussion boards that allow signatures, profile links, or relevant thread posts.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

## Homeowner lawn care forums

- aroundtheyard.com Forums – Very **focused** DIY lawn forum with sections like “Lawn Care Basics 101”; good for adding your blog in a signature and occasionally linking in detailed how‑to replies.[^7][^1]
- Lawn Care Forum – GardenWeb / Houzz – Long‑running homeowner lawn forum with nearly 18k posts; threads on maintenance, fertilizer, and overseeding where a well‑timed, on‑topic blog link can fit naturally.[^2]
- Lawn Mower Forum – Has a “Lawn Care \& Landscaping” section plus equipment and pro‑talk areas; you can participate in Q\&A and reference your blog when it directly expands on your answer.[^3]


## Pro / business‑oriented forums

- Lawn Service Forum Community – Large business forum with dedicated sections for commercial maintenance, pesticides/fertilizers, and even a “Web Site, Technology, Social Media” area ideal for discussing and showcasing your blog as a marketing asset.[^4]
- Landscape Juice Network – UK‑based but active professional landscape and lawn community; discussions on lawn advice and garden maintenance offer chances to share specialized articles if they’re relevant and not purely promotional.[^5]


## Smaller or emerging communities

- The Lawncast Forums – Forums tied to a lawn‑focused podcast, with categories like “General Lawn Care Discussion” and “Lawn Care Tips and Tricks” where niche, detailed blog posts can complement ongoing discussions.[^6]


## How to share your blog without spamming

- Lead with value: write full, specific answers, then add “wrote a more detailed breakdown here” with your blog link only when it clearly adds depth.
- Use profiles and signatures: many forums allow a website field or signature line; set your blog there so every helpful post passively promotes it.
- Match topics to threads: target threads that align tightly with your posts (e.g., cool‑season overseeding, fungus ID, irrigation tuning) so links feel organic, not bolted on.
